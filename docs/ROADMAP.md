# Roadmap

## 1. External architecture generalization

Before broad method claims, review a second open diffusion architecture with
disjoint development and confirmation inputs. A compact starting scope could
use three groups × three stages × W8/W6/W4. Check residual–geometry coupling and
frozen-score decision value before considering architecture-specific refitting.

Proposed review outcomes are GO for sufficiently reproduced coupling/decision
value, PARTIAL for ranking or measurement value alone, and STOP for unsupported
transfer. These are future protocol choices, not current scientific conclusions.

## 2. Real execution utility and quality

Evaluate an actual packed low-precision path with measured storage, resident
memory, latency and decoded/task quality. Fake-quantized theoretical saving bits
must not substitute for these endpoints. Keep quality and runtime tradeoffs
visible, including negative outcomes.

## 3. Allocation only after those checks

Interaction-aware matched budgets may be reviewed later; current single-cell
decision results do not establish joint composability or allocation quality.

Do not extend DiT-only precision-mechanism probing indefinitely, fit a more
complex detector to failed test outcomes, or erase the competitive Constant and
negative one-step detector. Mechanism/measurement and limited frozen decision
results remain useful without an allocation claim.
